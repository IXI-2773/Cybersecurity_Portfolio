# Adding Awards & Honors

The public page is `awards.html`. It currently has one intentional empty state;
no awards, images or verification claims have been invented.

For each entry, supply:

- Award or honor title, exactly as issued.
- Issuing school, organization or employer.
- Month/year or year received.
- One or two sentences explaining the recognition and your contribution.
- An image, certificate or event photo, if you want it public.
- An optional official verification or announcement link.

Replace the `honors-empty` section with the real entries when ready. Preserve
the surrounding navigation and the separate education/certifications section.
Use a concise image description and omit private certificate IDs or contact
details from any image unless you want them published. Do not manufacture
placeholder entries while waiting for material.
