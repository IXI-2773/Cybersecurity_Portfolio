03 / PRIVATE ENGINEERING CASE STUDY

RAI — Evidence-Aware Research Infrastructure

A high-level case study of private Python research tooling designed to distinguish source-backed findings from unsupported answers and to constrain the authority of automated workflows.

PythonProvenanceUncertaintyPermission boundariesHuman review

Problem context

Research systems can produce confident-sounding answers even when documents disagree, coverage is incomplete, or a source is misattributed. RAI focuses on making these failure conditions observable and on preventing an unsupported statement from silently becoming an authoritative decision.

Architecture principles

  • Evidence before release: tie a claim to supporting source material and identify missing support.
  • Conflicting evidence: retain disagreement rather than masking it as a single certain answer.
  • Provenance: make source attribution and transformations inspectable where feasible.
  • Bounded authority: separate research output from permission to run tools, alter another system or approve consequential changes.
  • Replaceable components: avoid unnecessary dependence on a specific model provider or library.

Engineering contribution

The work covers research-tooling architecture, bounded offline evaluation, evidence-handling rules, reviewability, and integration contracts that preserve another system's independent authority. The central goal is to reject unsupported conclusions without suppressing valid source-backed answers.

Cybersecurity relevance

Security analysts face similar questions: is this alert supported by telemetry, are sources contradictory, what can be concluded, and what authority is required to act? RAI's approach offers transferable design patterns for trustworthy analyst-assistance tools.

What is not claimed

RAI is not represented here as a proven autonomous SOC analyst, an activated production security agent, or a replacement for qualified human review.

Future portfolio work can demonstrate a small public evidence-validation exercise on artificial security alerts. No proprietary code or live research data is needed for that exercise.

Evidence status

Architecture & engineering context

Private design and implementation work establishes this as an engineering effort, but the public case study does not certify production deployment, autonomous reliability, security efficacy, or outcomes on real-world security datasets.

Disclosure: No private prompts, configuration, source excerpts, agent privileges, internal identifiers or research inputs are published.
