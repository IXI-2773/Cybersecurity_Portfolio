Enterprise Account Investigation

Correlated failed logons, a later successful sign-in and a privileged group change into one reviewable identity timeline.

Windows events · Identity triage · Python · LAB-ID-001

Completed result: five linked account events and three escalation findings.

The investigation question

Did the sequence justify escalation as a linked account event chain? I tested the temporal and identity relationships while keeping alternative administrative explanations visible.

From event to decision

  1. Bound the failure burst. Three Event 4625 failures occurred for one account and source in four minutes.
  2. Connected the success. Event 4624 recorded a sign-in from the same source three minutes later.
  3. Reviewed the privilege change. Event 4728 followed two minutes after the sign-in.
  4. Kept the conclusion proportionate. The chain warrants escalation, but an approved administrator or automation could explain part of it.

Recommended response

Validate the privileged change, preserve identity and host telemetry, review active sessions and recent account changes, and contain the account if authorization cannot be established.
