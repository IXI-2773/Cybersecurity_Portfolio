# Phishing Email Investigation

A planned case covering message provenance, suspicious links and a defensible analyst handoff.

Email headers · URL review · Incident reporting

A scoped project brief with a clear investigation question and deliverables.

Planned project · Not yet executed

## The scenario

A fictional employee receives an unexpected sign-in request. The proposed exercise will use a purpose-built email sample to investigate the sender, message route, displayed link and authentication results without opening a live suspicious attachment or site.

## The investigation plan

- Preserve the sample message and inspect its headers, sender fields and Message-ID.
- Compare the displayed destination with the actual link and review available email authentication results.
- Separate observed indicators from assumptions about malicious intent.
- Write a timeline and recommendation, with scope and missing evidence.

## What I intend to publish

A synthetic email sample, annotated header review, defanged indicator table, benign comparison case and a short analyst report. The report will explain which evidence supports the assessment and which questions remain unanswered.

This is a proposed project brief. There are no completed investigation results, screenshots or performance claims yet. They will be added after the work is performed.
