03 / ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Transferable skills from complex systems.

01

Bounded authority

Separate analysis and recommendations from permission to perform consequential actions. Design explicit approval and execution boundaries.

02

Traceable evidence

Preserve provenance and distinguish observed facts, inferred conclusions, and unanswered questions. Make results reviewable.

03

Focused verification

Use targeted tests and well-scoped controls to catch regressions without paying repeated whole-suite costs for narrow changes.

  1. Form a falsifiable hypothesis and define the evidence needed.
  2. Work in a safe, authorized environment.

  4. Document findings, caveats, and a defensible next action.
